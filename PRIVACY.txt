========================================================================
                      TECHSCRIPT PRIVACY POLICY
========================================================================
Last Updated: May 2026

TechScript is designed from the ground up to respect user privacy. As a 
modern, developer-oriented systems programming platform, we believe that 
your code, data, and workspace should remain entirely under your control.

1. 100% LOCAL EXECUTION & OFFLINE-FIRST
---------------------------------------
All components of the TechScript ecosystem—including the native VM, compiler, 
interpreter, and the TechScript Studio visual IDE—run 100% locally on your 
machine. 
- No telemetry, analytics trackers, or diagnostic reports are collected or 
  transmitted to external servers.
- No active internet connection is required to compile, build, run, or edit 
  your plain-English scripts.

2. WORKSPACE DATA PRESERVATION
------------------------------
Your workspace directories, source files (.txs, .tx), and compiled bytecode 
files (.txbc) are stored solely on your local storage drives. The installer 
creates registry file associations for your convenience, which can be cleanly 
removed at any time using the standard Windows Uninstaller.

3. INTEGRITY & SECURITY
-----------------------
TechScript installers and binaries are fully compiled from audited open-source 
Rust repositories. We do not bundle third-party offers, hidden services, 
adware, or remote update agents.

By installing and using TechScript, you enjoy a highly optimized, state-of-the-art 
development environment that honors your digital sovereignty.
